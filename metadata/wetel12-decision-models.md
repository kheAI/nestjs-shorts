**Description:**
A triage nurse judges and the hospital acts. A decision model works the same way: it answers closed questions (yes or no, a choice, a score) with a probability instead of writing a sentence, and a workflow decides what happens next.

How each answer feeds a condition, switch or router step, where a person comes in at the confidence threshold, and what is not built yet: no decision model is wired into Wetel today, and we have not measured speed or cost.
